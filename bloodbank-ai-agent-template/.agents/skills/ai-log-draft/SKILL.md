# AI Log Draft

Help the human keep their own account of AI use in `docs/ai-log.md`. Read the
log for its format and examples, but never write to, edit, or otherwise modify
that file. Provide a ready-to-review entry in chat.

## Procedure

1. At the end of a task completed with an agent, or when the human asks for an
   AI-log entry, read `docs/ai-log.md` to follow its current template and
   examples. Also use the conversation and verified task results as evidence.
2. Draft one entry in the format shown in the log:

   ```markdown
   ## YYYY-MM-DD: feature or task
   - Generated by AI: ...
   - Written by me: ...
   - Verified: ...
   - Decided: ...
   ```

3. Describe only what is supported by the conversation and actual results:
   - **Generated by AI:** what the agent produced and what the human requested;
     name the provider only if known.
   - **Written by me:** work the human said they wrote or rewrote, and why.
     If none is known, say that the human can fill this in; do not infer.
   - **Verified:** checks that were actually performed and their results,
     including failures or unverified behavior.
   - **Decided:** choices the human explicitly made and proposals they
     explicitly refused. Do not portray an agent's suggestion as a human
     decision.
4. If any details are unknown, mark them for the human to complete instead of
   guessing or claiming verification. Keep the entry specific, concise, and
   consistent with the existing examples.
5. Present the draft in chat for the human to copy or revise. Never use tools
   to insert it into `docs/ai-log.md`, and do not claim that the log was
   updated.
