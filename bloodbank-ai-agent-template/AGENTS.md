# Blood Bank

Blood-Bank project: an app that facilitate the bonding between the blood donator and the pacient for whom he or she wants to donate blood. This app will be made in plain HTML, JavaScript, CSS. Do not add libraries, give me the plan first.

## Non-standard

- No framework, libraries, build step, or server; open `login.html` directly.
- The test account in `js/login.js` is not a real account.
